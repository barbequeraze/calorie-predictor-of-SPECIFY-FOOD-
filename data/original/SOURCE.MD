# Dataset Source

- **Dataset:** Food Nutrition and Calories Dataset
- **Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amineipad/food-nutrition-dataset-for-calorie-classification
- **Author:** Amn Amine (Kaggle)
- **License:** No specific license stated. The Kaggle page notes
  "No privacy or licensing concerns." 

## Summary
- **Unit of analysis:** One row = one food item
- **Records:** 5,000
- **Features:** protein_g, carbs_g, fat_g, fiber_g, sugar_g
- **Target:** calories
- **Derived column (excluded):** calorie_class

## Notes
- Values are synthetically generated but preserve realistic nutritional
  relationships.
- No missing values, no duplicates, no privacy concerns.
- The dataset was used for academic purposes as part of a course project.
